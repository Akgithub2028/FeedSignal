# FeedSignal pilot and evaluation protocol after M0

Updated: 6 October 2026. **M0 uses public research; interviews and private-team exports are no longer completion prerequisites.** [Dataset catalogue](PUBLIC_DATASETS.md) and [research report](RESEARCH_REPORT.md) are the completed evidence. This protocol governs future M2 pilots and target-domain evaluation. No actual team dataset, delivered pilot brief, commitment or payment is recorded here.

## Development data and evaluation

Use the locally acquired Banking77 only for clearly labeled development/demo work. Keep original labels and dataset license; do not treat banking intents as SaaS opportunity, urgency, revenue or willingness-to-pay labels. Record exact input hashes and split/adapter versions. The public-data quality report describes rows and duplicates, not model or ingestion results.

For future real-team data, record: dataset ID, owning team, consent date/scope, permitted storage/providers, source/version, time coverage, raw conversations, unique accounts or unknown, de-identification, retention/deletion date, and reviewer. Keep private conversations and identities outside tracked public docs.

Each reviewed observation needs message/conversation ID, exact evidence span, original problem, request type, source revision, known account identity, uncertainty, multi-issue boundaries and proposed grouping. Record corroborating and contradictory evidence. Unknown accounts/revenue stay unknown; do not turn counts into an uncalibrated churn prediction.

Split by account/conversation and time before tuning, keeping duplicates/revisions together. Include quiet weeks, missing identities, internal replies, ambiguous requests, contradictions, irrelevant chatter and delivery retries. Public datasets cannot establish account-grouped performance when account IDs are absent. Record measured extraction, evidence accuracy, distinct-account counts, import outcomes and brief usefulness separately.

## Real pilot brief contract

A short reviewed brief should include:

- Coverage window, successful/failed sources, and missing data.
- Up to three opportunities, exact quotes/source links, unique account/conversation counts and contradictory evidence.
- Revenue source/freshness/coverage when known; no fabricated ARR or risk estimate.
- Proposed next action, uncertainty, owner and reviewable decision.
- A quiet-week outcome when evidence is insufficient rather than filler or invented trends.

Ask asynchronously whether it changes a decision or saves recurring work. A scheduled interview is optional. No delivery is authorized merely by writing this protocol; owner-approved customer access and outbound channels are required.
